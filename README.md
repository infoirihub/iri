# HalalBridge

**Research • Training • Certification**

A modern Halal industry solutions platform bringing together Halal research, professional training, certification services, applicable standards, and business inquiries.

## Project status
Initial website foundation is being developed. The first release will include a responsive homepage, department pages, standards directory, and inquiry forms.

## Departments
- Halal Research
- Halal Training
- Certification
- Contact & Inquiries

## Important note
Certification claims and certificate issuance must reflect the actual legal authority, accreditation scope, and approvals of the responsible certification body. The website must not imply authorization that has not been obtained.
