01 / FATWA & INQUIRY PORTAL

Ask a Researcher.

Submit questions concerning Shariah and technical Halal matters, including ingredient sources, food processing, additives, manufacturing methods and contemporary Halal issues.

A1

Shariah & Fiqh

Questions involving Islamic legal principles, contemporary applications and scholarly analysis.

A2

Ingredients & Food Processing

Ingredient origins, additives, enzymes, processing aids, production methods and documentation.

A3

Answered Inquiries Archive

Key questions and approved scholarly responses will be organised into a searchable archive as reviewed answers are published.

Only reviewed and authorised responses will be published.

Submissions are received for research review. A submitted inquiry is not automatically a formal fatwa or certification decision.

03 / ACADEMIC REPOSITORY

A repository for M.Phil and Ph.D level research on the Halal industry, Shariah, Islamic jurisprudence and food sciences.

Academic Abstracts

Each record will include the research title, abstract, keywords, degree level and research area.

Author & Institution

Author names, university or research institution, supervisor details where authorised, and year of completion.

View & Download

View or download full papers where authors or institutions have granted the necessary access and copyright permission.

Repository records and downloadable files will be published in phases after metadata and permissions are verified.
