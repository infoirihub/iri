FIND E NUMBER

E Number Halal Status Search

Enter an E number, for example E471, E120 or 422. You can also search by additive name, for example gelatine, or by a term, for example emulsifier, to see its explanation. Tap a result to see the full reason.

Halal Permissible on source groundsHaram Source forbidden, no permissible alternativeMashbooh Doubtful — check the source

This is general guidance based on the additive's typical source. It is not a formal fatwa for a specific product. Mashbooh means the source can be halal or haram: check whether the product is plant-based, synthetic, fish-based, or certified halal, or contact the manufacturer, because a version from pork or a non-halal animal source would be Haram for that product. Haram is used only where the source itself has no permissible alternative, such as an insect colour. Scholars differ on some codes, and those differences are noted in the reason where they matter.
