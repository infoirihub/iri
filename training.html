TRAINING PORTFOLIO

Learning pathways for people and organisations.

Halal Awareness

Foundational learning on Halal and Tayyib principles, ingredient concerns, contamination prevention and responsibilities across the supply chain.

Halal Internal Auditor

Understand audit principles, audit planning, evidence collection, nonconformity reporting and follow-up within a Halal management system.

Halal Lead Auditor

Advanced learning on audit leadership, audit-team coordination, risk-based approaches, reporting and professional audit conduct.

HACCP & Food Safety

Training on hazard analysis, critical control points, prerequisite programmes and food-safety management fundamentals.

GMP & Hygiene

Practical understanding of good manufacturing practices, sanitation, personnel hygiene, facility controls and documentation.

Standards Orientation

Introductory and focused sessions on relevant Pakistan, OIC/SMIIC, GSO and Malaysian Halal standards, subject to programme scope.
