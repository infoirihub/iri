LEARN QURAN ONLINE

Learn the Qur'an online, at your pace.

Live online Qur'an classes for children and adults — from Qaida to Hifz — taught by experienced teachers, in the language you are most comfortable with, at a time that suits you.

OUR PROGRAMMES

Four paths, one goal.

Qaida Quran

The foundation of Qur'an reading. Learn the Arabic letters, their sounds and joining, and the rules of reading — step by step, for complete beginners of any age.

Nazra Quran

Fluent recitation of the Holy Qur'an with Tajweed. Read with accuracy, beauty and confidence, guided verse by verse by your teacher.

Hifz Quran

Memorisation of the Holy Qur'an with a structured plan — new lesson, revision of recent lessons and revision of earlier portions — with steady, gentle progress at your own pace.

Islamic Tarbiyah

Essential Islamic knowledge and character-building alongside Qur'an learning — duas, salah, akhlaaq and everyday Islamic practice, for children and adults.
