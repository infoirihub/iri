HALALBRIDGE / PRIVACY POLICY

Your privacy, plainly explained.

Last updated: 6 November 2026. This policy covers the HalalBridge website only. Links to laboratories, standards bodies and other external sites are governed by those sites' own policies.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry through the site, you provide the details you type into the form: your full name, work email, and your message or question. Company or organisation, phone or WhatsApp number, service area, applicable standard, and product or service scope are optional or depend on the form you use.

The Find E Number search does not collect anything: the E-number database is loaded in your browser and your search stays on your device.

How we use it

We use your enquiry details only to respond to you, review your request, and, where required, contact the relevant standards body or institution on your behalf. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it goes

  • Enquiry forms: submissions are sent via FormSubmit to HalalBridge at contacthalalbridge@gmail.com.
  • WhatsApp: if you choose "Send via WhatsApp", your enquiry text is placed into a WhatsApp message to +923093873692, and is then handled under WhatsApp's own terms. Choosing WhatsApp is optional; the email form is the alternative.
  • Supabase (not yet connected): the admin dashboard is currently not connected to a database, so enquiries are not stored in Supabase today. If Supabase is connected in future, this section will be updated before that launch to say that laboratory-testing requests are stored there, and who can access them.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails until your request is resolved, and then for as long as you choose on the enquiry form (12 months by default), for follow-up, then delete them. Your choice is sent with your enquiry, so we know how long to keep it.

Your choices

To ask for a copy of your enquiry, a correction, or deletion, contact us at contacthalalbridge@gmail.com.

Other notes

  • The site does not offer accounts, logins, or online payments. Library purchases and subscriptions described on the site are arranged manually via WhatsApp.
  • The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and is hosted on GitHub Pages; those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address when pages load, under their own policies.

Questions about your data?

Write to contacthalalbridge@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp at +923093873692.
