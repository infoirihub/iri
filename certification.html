A TYPICAL CERTIFICATION PATHWAY
01Application & scopeDefine products, sites, activities and target markets.
02Document reviewReview ingredients, suppliers and relevant system documents.
03Audit & correctionComplete applicable audit activities and address findings.
04Independent decisionCertification decision is made by the authorised decision function.

This is a general overview, not a promise of certification. Certification decisions, certificates and accreditation claims belong only to the appropriately authorised and competent bodies.

RELEVANT FRAMEWORKS

Halal Standards

PS 3733, OIC/SMIIC 1, MS 1500 and applicable GSO requirements, depending on market and scope.

Food Safety

HACCP and ISO 22000 frameworks for food-safety hazard control and management systems.

Quality & Good Practice

ISO 9001 and GMP principles where relevant to the organisation and certification objective.
